# 3D floors

Floors that have a 3D model render as a Mapplic-style 3D map in the kiosk (`index.html`) and on the phone page (`m/`). The map turns 360° and tilts, has switchable layers, and shows each tenant's name or logo on its roof.

The 3D floors are drawn one by one in Illustrator. Until a floor has its model, `BSB_CONFIG.map3dOnly` (on) shows it empty with a short "coming soon" note instead of the old 2D drawing. **Every floor has its model** (October 2026):

| Floor | Model | Drawing (outside the repo) |
|---|---|---|
| `lg-ewalk`, `lg-penta` | `ewalk-LG.json`, `pentacity-LG.json` | `Ewalk/LG.svg`, `Penta/LG.svg` |
| `gf`, `ug` (both buildings) | `bsb-GF.json`, `bsb-UG.json`, joined from `ewalk-GF/UG.json` + `pentacity-GF/UG.json` | `maps mall/gf ewalk.svg`, `gf penta.svg`, `ug ewalk.svg`, `ug penta.svg` |
| `ff-penta`, `sf-penta` | `pentacity-FF.json`, `pentacity-SF.json` | `maps mall/ff penta.svg`, `sf penta.svg` |
| `ff-ewalk`, `sf-ewalk` | `ewalk-FF.json`, `ewalk-SF.json` | `maps mall/1f penta.svg` (it is E-Walk 1F), `2f ewalk.svg` |

**The CMS stays the source of truth.** Tenant names, logos, active/inactive, details, facilities and walking paths are edited in the CMS as before, and reach the 3D map through Publish. A 3D floor's CMS coordinates are the Illustrator drawing's own units, so every CMS map (graph editor, tenant location, facilities, kiosks) shows that floor as a top-down plan of the same drawing.

## What the kiosk shows on a 3D floor

- **Roofs.** A tenant linked to a building (`tenants.unit_shape_id`, "Pilih unit" in the CMS) puts its name on that roof, or its logo when it has a real one.
  - The roof shows the brand name, without the " · E-Walk LG" a multi-outlet brand gets.
  - A logo that is really a shop-front photo is detected and replaced by the name.
  - A building whose tenant is unpublished or permanently closed is blank (`floors[].unitsHidden` in `directory.json`).
- **Roof tones** follow Mapplic's demo maps: shops white, anchors (`kind: anchor`, large units) dark grey with white names, empty units and switched-off tenants light grey, on the khaki floor.
- **Roof edits.** The 3D editor can override a roof's name, logo, emboss/board style, roof colour (*Warna atap*: automatic / light / dark, one of ten tidy swatches, or any colour; also on unnamed units), size, rotation and position (`data/3d/edits/<floor>.json`: `tone`, `color` "#rrggbb" as the roof should look). An override wins over the CMS. Edits are live without Publish; a running kiosk reloads them every `BSB_CONFIG.map3dEditsPollSec` (60 s).
- **Map tenants.** A named building in the drawing with no CMS tenant still shows up: tappable, searchable, routed to the nearest corridor. Its id is `3d:<floor>:<unit>` and its category is guessed from the name. Create the CMS tenant for it in CMS > Peta 3D.
- **Facilities.** Icons come from the drawing's `Fasilitas` layer. Each icon is matched to the nearest CMS facility or lift/escalator stop of its kind within 25 m. Tapping it opens the route to that facility. A facility that exists only in the CMS gets an icon at its pin.
- **Pins and routes.**
  - The destination pin stands on the middle of the building's roof.
  - Routes end at the tenant's door node.
  - The route is drawn in 3D as a ribbon on the floor and shows faintly through walls.
- **Turning.** Two-finger twist, the ⟲ ⟳ buttons, or right-drag / shift-drag with a mouse. The compass button and the idle reset return to the default view. Names and logos stay put on the roofs while the map turns, laid out once for the default view as if printed there, so from the far side they read upside down.
- **Phone walking navigation.** On a 3D floor the map turns so the way ahead points up.

## Editing in the CMS

| Page | What it does for a 3D floor |
|---|---|
| **Peta 3D** | Status of each 3D floor and a "sync drawing ↔ CMS" panel:<br>• **Named buildings without a tenant:** link the CMS tenant whose name is similar (the drawing may spell it differently, e.g. "paper lunch" → Pepper Lunch), or create one (brand, category guessed, door pin, connected to the paths, unit linked). A second building drawn with the same name as a linked tenant (two Solaria units) shows that tenant without its own entry.<br>• **Buat fasilitas CMS** for drawn toilets, prayer rooms, ATMs and so on. Lifts and escalators are connectors; mark their stops in Eskalator & lift.<br>• **Perbarui denah dari gambar** after the drawing was converted again.<br>• **Sambung pin ke jalur**: see below.<br>• The embedded 3D editor for roof overrides. |
| **Editor graph** | Paths on the top-down plan:<br>• Nodes, edges, closures, schedules and route testing as before.<br>• An edge can be marked **Tangga**: walkable, but skipped in wheelchair mode (`graph_edges.type = 'stairs'`).<br>• **Sambung pin otomatis** connects tenant and facility pins to the current paths. |
| **Tenant → Lokasi** | **Pilih unit** links the building, **Tempatkan pin** places the door (snapped to the nearest corridor, as before), and **Sambung otomatis** does both from the linked building. |
| **Tenant → Info / Media** | Name, category, status, *Tampilkan di kiosk*, logo: all show on the roof after Publish. |
| **Lantai** | Focus box (the kiosk's default camera) and unit scale, as before. |

**Automatic connection** (`Map3d::reconnect`, `POST /api/admin/graph/reconnect`):

1. **The door.** The door goes on the side of the building that faces walkable floor in the drawing. An existing pin is moved onto the nearest such wall, because it records the real entrance.
2. **The path.** A path to the nearest corridor edge is found over the walkable floor: the drawing's slab minus buildings, facility rooms and the podium rim, on a 0.4 m grid. Its turns become nodes labelled `spur:<id>`.
3. **What it never uses.** It never runs through another shop's door spur.

A door that faces no walkable floor is connected in a straight line and reported. E-Walk LG's **Mako** is boxed in by J Deli, BSB Karaoke and Kidzilla in the drawing. Floors without a model fall back to the old straight snap.

## How it fits together

```
Illustrator plan (outside this repo: layers base, tenant, Fasilitas, optional void / skala)
  └─ design web (../pipeline/ai_svg_to_floor.py) or tools/3d/svg_to_3d.py ──► data/3d/<mall>-<floor>.json
       model in metres: origin, m_per_unit, artboard, rim, slab, units, facilities
       └─ register once: tools/3d/register_floor.py ──► data/3d/floors.json (fit onto the old 2D floor)
          (a floor both buildings share: tools/3d/merge_floor.py joins one drawing per building, then registers it)
            └─ rebase once: tools/3d/rebase_floor.php ──► CMS coordinates = drawing units, "frame": "plan"
                 └─ CMS (tenants, paths, facilities) ──► Publish ──► data/directory.json (+ unitShape, unitsHidden)
                 └─ 3D editor ──► data/3d/edits/<floor>.json, media/3d/ (roof overrides, live)
                      └─ js/scene3d.js + js/map3d.js render it in the kiosk and on the phone
```

### `data/3d/floors.json`

```json
{ "version": 1, "floors": { "lg-ewalk": {
    "model": "data/3d/ewalk-LG.json",
    "edits": "data/3d/edits/ewalk-LG.json",
    "frame": "plan",
    "view": { "azimuth": -10.5, "polar": 47 },
    "legacy": { "toMetres": [[...], [...]], "unitScale": 0.3598, "viewBox": [...], "rebased": "...", "ecc": 0.86 }
} } }
```

- **`frame: "plan"`.** The floor's CMS coordinates are the drawing's artboard units, so `metres = (x − origin) × m_per_unit` comes from the model itself. Converting the drawing again never needs a new registration as long as the artboard stays the same.
- **Older floors.** Floors not rebased yet have `toMetres` (fitted by `register_floor.py`) and a `tenants` map (unit → tenant) instead. The kiosk uses CMS links first, then that map.
- **`view`.** The default angle: fitted to the old drawing, so 3D and 2D floors face the same way.
- **`legacy`.** Records the transform used when the floor was rebased.
- **`parts`** (joined floors only). Per building: its model, where it sits in the joined model (`rot` degrees, `shift` metres) and its own fit onto the old floor (`toMetres`). `merge_floor.py --floor gf` rejoins from it; the rebase used the per-building fits.

### Code

- `js/map3d.js` (`B.MapView3D`) extends `B.MapView`, so `kiosk.js` and `phone.js` call the same API. The camera stays `{cx, cy, s}` in floor units; a 2×2 projection replaces the uniform scale. Before search and routing are built it merges the floor's model with the directory: roof names and logos, hidden buildings, map tenants, facility icons. A tap opens a tenant (`onTenantTap`) or a facility (`onAmenityTap`).
- `js/scene3d.js` (`B.Scene3D`) builds the scene from a model plus edits. It is shared by the kiosk, the phone and `editor3d.html`.
- `lib/Map3d.php` holds the registry and model helpers, the top-down plan SVG, tenant/facility import, walkable-floor connection and reconnection.
- `lib/Api/Map3dApi.php` serves:
  - `GET /api/3d/ping`;
  - `GET /api/admin/3d/floors`;
  - `POST /api/admin/3d/edits?floor=` and `/api/admin/3d/logo?name=` (the editor's save, with login, CSRF and `tenants.edit` / `media.upload`);
  - `POST /api/admin/3d/floors/{id}/plan`, `/import-units` and `/import-facilities`;
  - `POST /api/admin/graph/reconnect`.
- `lib/Snapshot.php` exports `tenants[].unitShape` and `floors[].unitsHidden`. The in-floor `stairs` edge type is new.
- `tools/3d/register_floor.py` fits a model onto its old 2D floor; `tools/3d/merge_floor.py` joins the two buildings' drawings of GF / UG into one model and registers it; `tools/3d/rebase_floor.php` moves the CMS data onto the drawing.
- On a joined floor every unit and facility carries `building` (ids `ewalk-GF-…`, `pentacity-GF-…`). Same-name matching (a second Solaria building, Peta 3D suggestions, the design web roofs) stays within one building, and tenants / facilities created from the drawing get its building.

## Adding a floor

1. **Draw** the floor in Illustrator with the same layers as E-Walk LG:
   - `base`: the mall outline.
   - `tenant`: one closed shape per unit, with the object name set to the tenant name. A shape named like a facility ("toilet-sf", "eskalator-trn-cnc") is taken as that facility; one-letter booth codes ("h", "c") and Illustrator's default names ("<Path>") count as unnamed.
   - `Fasilitas`: Toilet, Lift, Escalator / Eskalator, Tangga, Musholla, ATM and so on. An unnamed shape is skipped.
   - Optional: `void`, `bolong` or `kosongan` (atrium openings), `other` (named rooms that are not shops, e.g. lobbies), `skala`.

   Keep the file **outside** this repo; a leasing plan placed in it is never read or committed.
2. **Convert** it on the design web (which also writes the model here), or here:
   ```
   python3 tools/3d/svg_to_3d.py "/path/to/Ewalk/GF.svg" --mall ewalk --floor GF --scale 0.1255
   ```
   The scale comes from a `skala` line, `--area "Some Tenant=120"`, or `--scale` (metres per Illustrator unit). The `maps mall/` drawings have neither, so each was fitted onto its building's LG drawing (same walls on every floor; the passenger lifts stack within 2–5 m). Scales used:

   | | LG | GF | UG | 1F | 2F |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | Pentacity | 0.24152 (areas) | 0.16181 | 0.15762 | 0.16602 | 0.16900 (on 1F's walls) |
   | E-Walk | 0.15188 (areas) | 0.12550 | 0.10928 | 0.11130 | 0.11111 |

   Keep the drawing's artboard when it is edited, and convert it again with the same scale.
3. **Register** it on its kiosk floor once. This fits the drawing onto the old 2D floor so the existing CMS data can be moved (needs Chrome, numpy, opencv):
   ```
   python3 tools/3d/register_floor.py --model data/3d/pentacity-FF.json --kiosk-floor ff-penta --check /tmp/check.png
   ```
   - Tenant names are matched loosely ("xxi-ewalk-ff" = Cinema XXI, "X8" = X8), and the rough fit pulls each tenant pin onto its own unit, since pins stand at the door.
   - `--shape-from lg-ewalk --outline` fits the building outline instead of names, using the old drawing style of another fitted floor of the same building. E-Walk 1F and 2F were registered this way: their few tenants are anchors with the pin at the entrance. `--no-refine` skips the corridor alignment (E-Walk 2F has almost no corridor).
   - Check the image, then link obvious matches the names missed in `floors.json` (`tenants`: unit → tenant) or later in CMS > Peta 3D.
4. **Rebase** the CMS data onto the drawing. It moves nodes, pins, facilities, kiosks, events and the focus box, installs the top-down plan and keeps the floor's walking cost:
   ```
   php tools/3d/rebase_floor.php --floor ff-penta --check /tmp/periksa.svg   # dry run + review image
   php tools/3d/rebase_floor.php --floor ff-penta --apply
   php tools/3d/rebase_floor.php --floor ff-penta --tidy --apply             # doors onto walls, corridors ≥ 1.2 m from shops
   php tools/3d/rebase_floor.php --floor ff-penta --focus --apply            # camera focus = shops + corridors (per building on GF / UG)
   ```
   `--tidy` was left out on E-Walk 1F and 2F: their old paths run through Matahari and across the atrium.
5. **In the CMS:**
   - Peta 3D → link the suggested tenants, create tenants for the remaining drawn units, create the drawn facilities.
   - Editor graph → check the paths and **Sambung pin otomatis** (or Peta 3D → Sambung pin ke jalur → Semua).
   - Publish.

After re-converting a drawing, use **Perbarui denah dari gambar** in Peta 3D, or `rebase_floor.php --plan-only --apply`. Unit ids follow the object names and their order in Illustrator, so a renamed or re-ordered shape gets a new id; the refresh matches the old plan's units to the new model by shape (overlap ≥ 0.5) and moves CMS unit links and roof edits along (e.g. `LG-luca` → `LG-luuca`). Facilities created from the drawing take a gender from their name ("laki laki", "pria" / "perempuan", "wanita") and are named accordingly (Musholla Pria / Wanita). `register_floor.py` refuses floors that are already on the plan frame.

### Floors both buildings share (GF, UG)

The CMS has one GF and one UG for both buildings, drawn as one drawing per building. `merge_floor.py` joins them:

```
python3 tools/3d/merge_floor.py --floor ug --part ewalk=data/3d/ewalk-UG.json --part pentacity=data/3d/pentacity-UG.json --check /tmp/ug.png
php tools/3d/rebase_floor.php --floor ug --check /tmp/periksa.svg   # then --apply, --tidy --apply, --focus --apply
```

- Each drawing is fitted onto the old floor from its own building's tenant names. E-Walk keeps its metres; Pentacity is placed beside it by a rigid move (rotation + shift), because both drawings are already in metres.
- The joined model `bsb-<FLOOR>.json` has one slab per building, prefixed ids and a new artboard around both buildings. The outdoor area between them is not drawn; the outdoor paths stay as edges.
- The old shared drawings are not one scale: on GF, E-Walk is about 20 % off against Pentacity. So the rebase moves each building's CMS points with that building's own fit, and blends the two for points in between.
- GF's placement was taken from UG (the buildings don't move between floors), carried through each building's LG drawing: `--place pentacity=56.3389,-250.958,-108.336`. UG's own fit agreed with both drawings' scales to 0.1 %.
- After re-converting one building's drawing, rejoin it with `python3 tools/3d/merge_floor.py --floor gf`, which keeps the placements and the CMS frame, then refresh the plan. The design web's converter does this rejoin itself.
- **Surroundings (kawasan).** GF also shows the land, roads, medians, ruko blocks and parking around the malls, drawn in `maps mall gf dan ug/gf ewalk penta dan kawasan.svg` (groups `mall/ewalk`, `mall/penta`, `gedung`, `base`, `other`). `python3 tools/3d/kawasan.py --floor gf --svg "<that file>"` writes them into the model as `context`.
  - Each mall of that drawing is fitted onto the model's mall by its named units. The surroundings follow both fits, so the malls, CMS data and routes stay as they are.
  - The two malls in that drawing are not one scale (about 14 % apart), so the ruko block nearest Pentacity bends slightly.
  - `merge_floor.py` keeps the surroundings on a rejoin. Refresh the plan afterwards.
  - UG's combined drawing has no surroundings, and its E-Walk part is incomplete, so UG is unchanged.

## Running locally

```
php -S 127.0.0.1:8000 tools/dev_router.php      # needs PHP 8 + MariaDB with the bsb_directory database
```

`tools/dev_router.php` mimics the production `.htaccess`: `/api/*` goes to the API, server folders are denied, the CMS gets its CSP, and HTML/JS/CSS/JSON are served no-store. `../buka-kiosk-3d.command` starts MariaDB and this server and opens the kiosk and CMS > Peta 3D.

| What | URL |
|---|---|
| Kiosk | http://localhost:8000/?attract=0&kiosk=gf-ewalk-left |
| Phone | http://localhost:8000/m/?k=gf-ewalk-left&t=kidzilla-lg-ewalk&a=0 |
| CMS | http://localhost:8000/cms/ (Peta 3D, Editor graph, Tenant) |
| 3D editor alone | http://localhost:8000/editor3d.html |

Without PHP, `python3 tools/3d/serve3d.py --port 8000` serves the kiosk from its static snapshot and lets the 3D editor save roof edits only.

## Connected to the design web

This repo is fed by the design web: the folder that contains this repo, with `preview.html` and `pipeline/`.

- **Model.** Converting a floor there also writes `data/3d/<mall>-<floor>.json` here, without the internal reference plan.
- **Edits.** Both editors read and write the same edits file.
- **CMS data.** The design web preview shows the published CMS names, logos and hidden buildings too, so it looks like the kiosk.

## Deploying

The rebase, the new CMS tenants and facilities, and the stairs edges live in the **database**:

1. Run `db/migrate_2026_10_3d.sql` on the server database. It adds the `stairs` edge type and marks the GF E-Walk stairs.
2. Bring this local database's 3D floor changes across (every floor). Use a full dump of the local database (`mariadb-dump --single-transaction bsb_directory > bsb_directory.sql`, imported with phpMyAdmin), or run the same rebase and imports there. `tools/export_cpanel_sql.php` rebuilds the database from `directory.json` and loses unit links, so it does not fit here.
3. Deploy the files:
   - `js/`, `lib/`, `cms/`, `editor3d.html`, `config.js` (`APP_BUILD` bumped);
   - `data/3d/`, `media/floors/`, `media/pub/floors/`, `media/3d/`, `assets/logos/`.
4. Publish from the CMS.
